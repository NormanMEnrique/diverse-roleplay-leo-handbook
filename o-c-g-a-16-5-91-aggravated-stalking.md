# O.C.G.A. § 16-5-91 — Aggravated Stalking

## Charge Information

> **Felony**  
> **Class:** Class B  
> **Fine:** $30,000  
> **Jail:** 55 Minutes  
> **Points:** 0

## What the Law Means

Commits stalking in violation of a qualifying court order, bond condition, probation/parole condition, or other statutory restriction. Use this charge only when the required act against another person and the required intent, knowledge, recklessness, injury, or aggravating circumstance can be articulated.

## What You Need to Charge

Establish the conduct described by the offense and every required fact that makes the conduct unlawful. Document identity, location, intent or knowledge when required, and any relevant victim, property, vehicle, weapon, substance, value, quantity, license, permit, prior conviction, official order, sign, signal, or aggravating circumstance. Specifically establish: Commits stalking in violation of a qualifying court order, bond condition, probation/parole condition, or other statutory restriction. Use this charge only when the required act against another person and the required intent, knowledge, recklessness, injury, or aggravating circumstance can be articulated.

## Examples

**Example 1:** An officer observes or establishes facts matching the conduct described by this offense: Commits stalking in violation of a qualifying court order, bond condition, probation/parole condition, or other statutory restriction. **Example 2:** The investigation provides observations, statements, records, video, physical evidence, or other facts supporting each required part of the offense.

## When NOT to Charge

Do not apply this charge when a required element is missing. Suspicion, association, or conduct that merely resembles the offense is not enough by itself.

## What to Document

- Exact conduct observed or established
- Location and circumstances
- Facts establishing intent, knowledge, recklessness, or status when required
- Relevant victim, property, vehicle, weapon, substance, value, quantity, license, permit, sign, signal, or roadway condition
- Supporting statements, witnesses, records, photographs, video, or physical evidence

### Additional Enforcement Notes

LEO applicability: identify the victim and suspect, describe the act and injuries/threat, establish the required mental state from statements or circumstances, and document any weapon, protected-victim status, serious bodily injury, death, restraint, sexual conduct, or other aggravator required by the offense.
