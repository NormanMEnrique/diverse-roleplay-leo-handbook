# O.C.G.A. § 10-1-490 — Registration of Business / Trade Name

## Charge Information

> **Misdemeanor**  
> **Class:** Class C  
> **Fine:** $5,000  
> **Jail:** 10 Minutes  
> **Points:** 0

## What the Law Means

Requires a person, partnership, corporation, or other business operating under a trade name or business name covered by the statute to properly register that name with the appropriate government authority. For purposes, this establishes the underlying registration requirement for businesses operating under a registered business or trade name.

Do not apply this charge when a required element is missing. Suspicion, association, or conduct that merely resembles the offense is not enough by itself.

## What to Document

- Exact conduct observed or established
- Location and circumstances

- Relevant victim, property, vehicle, weapon, substance, value, quantity, license, permit, sign, signal, or roadway condition
- Supporting statements, witnesses, records, photographs, video, or physical evidence

### Additional Enforcement Notes

LEO applicability: use this entry to establish the underlying business-registration requirement. Confirm the business is actively operating, identify the owner or responsible operator, determine the business or trade name being used, and verify through the applicable government/business records that the required registration was not completed. When enforcement is based specifically on operating without the required trade-name registration, O.C.G.A. § 10-1-493 is the related misdemeanor penalty provision in this code.
