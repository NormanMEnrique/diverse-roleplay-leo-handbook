# O.C.G.A. § 10-1-493 — Failure to Register Business / Trade Name

## Charge Information

> **Misdemeanor**  
> **Class:** Class C  
> **Fine:** $10,000  
> **Jail:** 15 Minutes  
> **Points:** 0

## What the Law Means

Knowingly operates or conducts a business under a trade name when the business is required to register that trade name and the required registration has not been completed. For purposes, this charge applies when a civilian continues operating a business that is legally required to be registered with the State or appropriate government authority.

## When NOT to Charge

Do not apply this charge when a required element is missing. Suspicion, association, or conduct that merely resembles the offense is not enough by itself.

## What to Document

- Exact conduct observed or established
- Location and circumstances
- Facts establishing intent, knowledge, recklessness, or status when required
- Relevant victim, property, vehicle, weapon, substance, value, quantity, license, permit, sign, signal, or roadway condition
- Supporting statements, witnesses, records, photographs, video, or physical evidence

### Additional Enforcement Notes

LEO applicability: confirm that the business or trade name was legally required to be registered, identify the person responsible for operating the business, verify that the required registration was not completed or maintained, and document evidence that the business was actively operating. Do not use this charge merely because paperwork is delayed when no registration requirement applies.
