# O.C.G.A. § 15-21A-8 (Under $10,000) — Failure to Remit Indigent Defense Funds — Under $10,000

## Charge Information

> **Misdemeanor**  
> **Class:** Class A  
> **Fine:** $20,000  
> **Jail:** 15 Minutes  
> **Points:** 0

## What the Law Means

A clerk, officer, or agent receiving covered funds to knowingly fail to pay them over after notice that the funds are delinquent, where the amount is under $10,000. Apply this charge only when the civilian's conduct matches each material requirement stated by the offense.
