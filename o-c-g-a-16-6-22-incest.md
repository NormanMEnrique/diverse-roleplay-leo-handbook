# O.C.G.A. § 16-6-22 — Incest

## Charge Information

> **Felony**  
> **Class:** Class A  
> **Fine:** $50,000  
> **Jail:** 40 Minutes  
> **Points:** 0

## What the Law Means

Engages in sexual intercourse or sodomy with a person known to be within a prohibited degree of familial relationship. Apply this charge only when the civilian's conduct matches each material requirement stated by the offense.

Do not apply this charge when a required element is missing. Suspicion, association, or conduct that merely resembles the offense is not enough by itself.
