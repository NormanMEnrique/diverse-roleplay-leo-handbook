# O.C.G.A. § 16-9-55 — Medical Assistance Fraud

## Charge Information

> **Felony**  
> **Class:** Class B  
> **Fine:** $30,000  
> **Jail:** 40 Minutes  
> **Points:** 0

## What the Law Means

Knowingly makes false statements, claims, or representations to obtain or cause payment of medical-assistance benefits not lawfully due. The offense generally requires a knowing or intentional false representation, fraudulent document, concealment, or prohibited financial/tax act.

## What You Need to Charge

Establish the conduct described by the offense and every required fact that makes the conduct unlawful. Document identity, location, intent or knowledge when required, and any relevant victim, property, vehicle, weapon, substance, value, quantity, license, permit, prior conviction, official order, sign, signal, or aggravating circumstance. Specifically establish: Knowingly makes false statements, claims, or representations to obtain or cause payment of medical-assistance benefits not lawfully due. The offense generally requires a knowing or intentional false representation, fraudulent document, concealment, or prohibited financial/tax act.

## When NOT to Charge

Do not apply this charge when a required element is missing. Suspicion, association, or conduct that merely resembles the offense is not enough by itself.

## What to Document

- Exact conduct observed or established
- Location and circumstances
- Facts establishing intent, knowledge, recklessness, or status when required
- Relevant victim, property, vehicle, weapon, substance, value, quantity, license, permit, sign, signal, or roadway condition
- Supporting statements, witnesses, records, photographs, video, or physical evidence

### Additional Enforcement Notes

LEO applicability: identify the false statement/document/transaction, what made it false or unauthorized, who relied on or was affected by it, the suspect's knowledge or intent, and any amount/value/filing duty that changes the grade of the offense.
