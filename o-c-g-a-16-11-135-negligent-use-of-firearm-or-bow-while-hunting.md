# O.C.G.A. § 16-11-135 — Negligent Use of Firearm or Bow While Hunting

## Charge Information

> **Misdemeanor**  
> **Class:** Class A  
> **Fine:** $20,000  
> **Jail:** 15 Minutes  
> **Points:** 0

## What the Law Means

Uses a firearm or bow while hunting in a statutorily prohibited negligent or intoxicated manner. The charge applies only when the civilian's possession, carrying, transfer, discharge, or use of the weapon/equipment is prohibited under the listed circumstances.

## What You Need to Charge

Establish the conduct described by the offense and every required fact that makes the conduct unlawful. Document identity, location, intent or knowledge when required, and any relevant victim, property, vehicle, weapon, substance, value, quantity, license, permit, prior conviction, official order, sign, signal, or aggravating circumstance. Specifically establish: Uses a firearm or bow while hunting in a statutorily prohibited negligent or intoxicated manner. The charge applies only when the civilian's possession, carrying, transfer, discharge, or use of the weapon/equipment is prohibited under the listed circumstances.

## When NOT to Charge

Do not apply this charge when a required element is missing. Suspicion, association, or conduct that merely resembles the offense is not enough by itself.

## What to Document

- Exact conduct observed or established
- Location and circumstances
- Facts establishing intent, knowledge, recklessness, or status when required
- Relevant victim, property, vehicle, weapon, substance, value, quantity, license, permit, sign, signal, or roadway condition
- Supporting statements, witnesses, records, photographs, video, or physical evidence

### Additional Enforcement Notes

LEO applicability: identify and recover/describe the weapon when possible, establish possession or control, license/permit or prohibited-person status when relevant, location, manner of carry/use, and any victim, discharge, serial-number, or aggravating circumstance required.
